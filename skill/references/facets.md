# HEXACO facets: what high and low mean

Every trait is a 0 to 100 score where **50 is an average person**. Only distinctive
values (roughly below 35 or above 65) produce anything in the compiled output, so
move only the facets that actually define this character. Think in contrasts: a
memorable persona is usually a few facets pushed in tension with each other, not
every slider cranked.

Six domains, four facets each, plus the interstitial `altruism`.

## Honesty-Humility

Sincere, fair, unassuming, uninterested in status, versus sly, greedy, entitled.

| facet | high | low |
|---|---|---|
| sincerity | genuine, says what they think | will flatter or manipulate to get ahead |
| fairness | plays fair even unseen | bends rules and cuts corners for gain |
| greed_avoidance | indifferent to wealth, luxury, status | wants money and status |
| modesty | unassuming, sees self as ordinary | sees self as superior, entitled |

Note: low `sincerity` is one of the three ingredients of sycophancy.

## Emotionality

Anxious, sentimental, needs support, versus tough and independent.

| facet | high | low |
|---|---|---|
| fearfulness | fears danger and pain | unafraid, unmoved by physical risk |
| anxiety | worries, stressed easily | calm, hard to rattle |
| dependence | wants reassurance and support | self-reliant, handles problems alone |
| sentimentality | forms deep bonds, easily moved | emotionally detached |

Note: high `dependence` is one of the three ingredients of sycophancy.

## Extraversion

Socially confident and lively, versus reserved and withdrawn.

| facet | high | low |
|---|---|---|
| social_self_esteem | feels liked, at ease socially | feels awkward or unpopular |
| social_boldness | confident, speaks up, leads | shy, holds back |
| sociability | enjoys company and conversation | prefers solitude |
| liveliness | energetic, enthusiastic, cheerful | low-key, subdued |

## Agreeableness

Forgiving, gentle, patient, versus critical, stubborn, hot-tempered.

| facet | high | low |
|---|---|---|
| forgivingness | lets go of grudges | holds grudges |
| gentleness | mild, lenient in judgment | harsh, critical |
| flexibility | compromises, cooperates | stubborn, will not budge |
| patience | keeps temper under provocation | quick to anger |

Note: high `flexibility` is one of the three ingredients of sycophancy. To make an
AI stop caving, lower flexibility and raise sincerity.

## Conscientiousness

Organized, diligent, careful, versus sloppy, lazy, impulsive.

| facet | high | low |
|---|---|---|
| organization | orderly, tidy, structured | messy, disorganized |
| diligence | hardworking, finishes what it starts | does the minimum |
| perfectionism | thorough, checks details | tolerant of errors |
| prudence | deliberate, controls impulses | impulsive, takes risks |

## Openness to Experience

Curious, creative, unconventional, versus conventional and practical.

| facet | high | low |
|---|---|---|
| aesthetic_appreciation | drawn to art, beauty, nature | indifferent to art |
| inquisitiveness | curious, digs, asks | incurious |
| creativity | imaginative, innovative | prefers proven ways |
| unconventionality | open to odd or radical ideas | conventional, likes the familiar |

## Altruism (interstitial)

Not part of a single domain. Sympathetic and helpful versus hard-hearted.

| facet | high | low |
|---|---|---|
| altruism | genuinely moved by others' wellbeing | indifferent to others' wellbeing |

## The sycophancy pattern

Sycophancy is not a mood, it is a facet configuration: **high flexibility, low
sincerity, high dependence**. If the user complains that their AI agrees with
everything or flatters them, that is the pattern to invert. The `honest-sparring`
persona is the canonical inversion.
